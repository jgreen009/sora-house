The room

The Dune Room sits at the back of the house, where the land rises into the dune grass. It is a small, calm room with one deep window, a low bed and a bench that fits two people and a book. In the morning the light comes in sideways and the garden is the first thing you see.

We designed these four rooms for the people who came to Sora House to do nothing in particular. There is a chair, a table, a shelf of books that guests have left behind, and a deep sill to sit on. The plaster is warm to the touch by ten in the morning. From the bed you can hear the sea, but only just — which is the point.
