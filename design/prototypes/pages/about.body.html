01 / The idea

Less noise.
More attention.

The starting point is a small house. A courtyard, twelve rooms, and an open horizon. Enough space to be alone, and a table to come back to.

At Sora House, the architecture does much of the talking. Pale stone, warm oak, and a quiet courtyard make space for the hours between plans.

02 / Architecture

Low, curved,
and open to the light.

The house is one storey, arranged around a sheltered courtyard. Deep arches frame the view and make a changing pattern of shade.

There are no grand gestures in the plan. The interest is in the spaces between things: a doorway and its shadow, a room and its terrace, the house and the coast.
