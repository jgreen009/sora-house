Planning your stay

Reservations

Online reservations are not yet available. Choosing a room and dates or viewing stay details does not reserve a room, hold inventory, or create a booking. No payment is taken on this website.

Rates

Nightly rates are shown in euro and include breakfast. The displayed stay total is the nightly room rate multiplied by the number of nights. Availability and final booking terms must be confirmed before a reservation is made.

Room capacity

Stays can be planned for one to fourteen nights, with one room selected at a time. Dune, Courtyard, and Atlantic Rooms each sleep two guests; the Sora Suite sleeps three.

Enquiries

Online enquiries are currently unavailable. The enquiry form does not send a message.
