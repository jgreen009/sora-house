## Production approach

Use the Higgsfield MCP connection for the requested imagery. Preserve the warm limestone, deep rounded arches, lime plaster, natural oak, washed linen, low dunes, and muted Atlantic palette. Natural daylight, straight architectural verticals, realistic texture, and restrained furniture establish continuity. Do not add unsupported operating claims or on-location shoot claims to public copy.

Generation provenance is internal production information. Images were generated, not photographed on location. Asset contact sheets and room corrections were reviewed in isolated contexts. Recheck intended crops whenever compositions change; use one page/viewport per review task and respect the image limits in AGENTS.md.
