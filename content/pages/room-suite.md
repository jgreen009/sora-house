The room

The Sora Suite occupies the western end of the house. It has a bedroom, a separate lounge with its own fireplace, and a wide view of the Atlantic.

We built one suite rather than two because the plan only works once. The lounge has a low fire for the evenings when the wind turns, and a long sofa that sleeps one if you need it. In the morning the suite is quiet enough to hear the water arrive on the sand, which takes about twenty minutes and is worth staying in bed for.
