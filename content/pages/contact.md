## Before your stay

Button: How many nights can I stay?

Choose a stay of one to fourteen nights. One room can be selected at a time.

Button: How many guests can share a room?

The Dune, Courtyard, and Atlantic Rooms each sleep two guests. The Sora Suite sleeps three.

Button: Is breakfast included?

Breakfast is included in every nightly room rate shown.

Button: Can I reserve a room online?

Online reservations are not yet available. You can explore rooms, choose dates, and review the price of your stay.

Button: Which currency are rates shown in?

All room rates are shown in euro, per room, per night.

Button: Where can I compare the rooms?

The rooms page compares each room's floor area, outlook, guest capacity, and nightly rate.
