The room

The Atlantic Rooms are the widest of the four categories and face west across open sand. Each has a terrace deep enough to eat on, a window that runs almost the length of the wall, and the particular quality of light you only get an hour before sunset.

We kept these rooms deliberately plain so that the view does the work. The walls are unpainted lime plaster. The floor is wide oak boards with a rug woven in a village twenty kilometres inland. The terrace has two chairs and nothing else, because at this hour you will want to be facing the water. At night the curtains are heavy and the sea disappears behind them.
