01 / The Sora Table

## Good company.A longer lunch.

The dining room is arranged around one long oak table. Simple ceramics, linen, and daylight give it the feel of a room used every day.

The table is a reason to gather, sit a little longer, and leave the rest of the day open. Simple materials and daylight keep the room close to its surroundings.

02 / Coastal Paths

## Follow the light.Leave the rest.

The landscape is the other half of the house. Low dunes, marram grass, and a long Atlantic horizon shape the setting.

The coast asks very little of you. Take time to look at the grass, the sand, and the light changing on the water.

03 / Slow Rituals

## A little spaceto do nothing.

A low lamp. Stone warmed by daylight. Linen, water, and a view of the courtyard. The room makes fewer demands on your attention.

A simple place to pause, with the courtyard just beyond the window.
