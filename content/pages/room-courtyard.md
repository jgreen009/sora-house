The room

The Courtyard Rooms open onto the planted heart of the house. They are larger than the Dune Rooms and lit from above rather than from the side, which makes them cool in the middle of the day and warm in the evening.

The courtyard was the first thing we built. It is a square of local stone with three olives, a bench and a shallow water rill that runs in winter. Around it, the three Courtyard Rooms each have a wide window onto it. Guests often take breakfast here, though we will always bring it to your door if you would rather stay put.
