The collection

The difference between our rooms is the view, the amount of morning light, and how close the sea is. That is all.

We keep the rooms simple on purpose. Linen, natural materials, and simple fittings connect the four room types; what changes is the view and the space around it. If you are choosing between two, the deciding question is usually how much of the day you want to spend looking out.

## Before you arrive

### Length of stay

Choose a stay of one to fourteen nights. Online reservations are not yet available.

### Breakfast

Breakfast is included in every room rate.

### Getting here

Sora House is on the Atlantic coast of Portugal. Dunes, open water, and Atlantic light surround the house.

### Guests and room capacity

Each room sleeps two guests; the Sora Suite sleeps three. Please confirm any additional requirements before reserving.
