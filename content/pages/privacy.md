# Privacy

How information is handled on this website.

Your information

## Enquiries

Online enquiries are currently unavailable. Information entered in the enquiry form is not sent or saved by this website.

## Stay details

Your room choice, dates, guest count, and stay reference are included in the address of the stay-details page. They may remain in your browser history or in a link you share. Names, email addresses, and notes are not included in that address.

## Website storage

This website does not currently use analytics, cookies, account storage, or a connected reservation service. Fonts and images are served with the website.
