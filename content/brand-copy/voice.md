# Sora House — brand voice

Plain, specific, quietly inviting British English. Speak to the guest in the second person where useful. Let architecture, natural materials, daylight, rooms and the coast carry the story. Keep claims tied to established project content and canonical room data.

Use short active labels: Explore the room, Plan your stay, View stay details, Read the story. State the actual outcome of a control and the actual availability of online services. Avoid unsupported superlatives, urgency, awards, testimonials, staff identities and operating details.

The complete positioning, manifesto and vocabulary are maintained in design/brand/brand-identity.md. Public page copy exports live in content/pages/ and can be refreshed with node tools/export-copy.mjs.
